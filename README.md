## Practices

- [ABCDE Method](https://www.briantracy.com/blog/time-management/the-abcde-list-technique-for-setting-priorities/) - Task prioritization technique by Brian Tracy that categorizes tasks into 5 categories (A through E) based on importance and consequences. Featured in 'Eat That Frog!' as a core productivity principle. ([Read more](/details/abcde-method.md)) `Prioritization` `Productivity` `Task Management` `Decision Making`
- [Covey Time Management Matrix](https://www.franklincovey.com/) - A four-quadrant framework for prioritizing time and tasks by urgency and importance, created by Stephen Covey in The Seven Habits of Highly Effective People, emphasizing focus on important but not urgent activities. ([Read more](/details/covey-time-management-matrix.md)) `Prioritization` `Time Management` `strategic-planning`
- [Deep Work](https://calnewport.com/deep-work-rules-for-focused-success-in-a-distracted-world/) - Productivity philosophy by Cal Newport defined as focusing without distraction on cognitively demanding tasks. Enables quickly mastering complicated information and producing quality results in less time through 90-minute focus sessions. ([Read more](/details/deep-work.md)) `Productivity` `Focus` `Concentration` `Learning`
- [Don't Break the Chain (Seinfeld Strategy)](https://hubstaff.com/blog/dont-break-the-chain/) - Habit-building method attributed to Jerry Seinfeld using visual tracking on a calendar. Mark each completed day with an X to create a chain; motivation comes from not breaking the growing streak. ([Read more](/details/dont-break-the-chain.md)) `Habits` `Consistency` `Visualization` `Motivation`
- [Eat That Frog](https://www.briantracy.com/blog/time-management/the-truth-about-frogs/) - Time management method by Brian Tracy based on tackling your biggest, most important task first thing each morning. The book has sold over 450,000 copies and been translated into 23 languages. ([Read more](/details/eat-that-frog.md)) `Productivity` `Methodology` `Prioritization` `Procrastination`
- [Eisenhower Matrix](https://www.eisenhower.me/eisenhower-matrix/) - Productivity and prioritization framework that categorizes tasks by urgency and importance into four quadrants. Based on President Eisenhower's decision-making principles, later popularized by Stephen Covey. ([Read more](/details/eisenhower-matrix.md)) `Productivity` `Prioritization` `Decision Making` `Planning`
- [Getting Things Done (GTD)](https://gettingthingsdone.com/) - Comprehensive time management system by David Allen consisting of five steps: capture, clarify, organize, reflect, and engage. An all-in-one system of lists and calendars for both work and personal life. ([Read more](/details/getting-things-done.md)) `Productivity` `Methodology` `Task Management` `Organization`
- [Getting Things Done (GTD)](https://gettingthingsdone.com/) - A comprehensive personal productivity system developed by David Allen that alleviates overwhelm and instills focus, clarity, and confidence by moving tasks out of the mind into an external system. ([Read more](/details/getting-things-done-gtd.md)) `Productivity` `workflow` `Task Management`
- [GTD Weekly Review](https://gettingthingsdone.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/Weekly_Review_Checklist.pdf) - Keystone habit of Getting Things Done system by David Allen. Weekly 15-20 minute ritual to get clear, get current, and get creative. Maintains system integrity and provides clarity for the week ahead. ([Read more](/details/gtd-weekly-review.md)) `Gtd` `Reflection` `Planning` `Review`
- [Ivy Lee Method](https://jamesclear.com/ivy-lee) - 100+ year-old productivity technique by consultant Ivy Lee involving listing and ranking six key tasks daily. Used by Charles Schwab's Bethlehem Steel executives to dramatically improve productivity through single-tasking. ([Read more](/details/ivy-lee-method.md)) `Prioritization` `Productivity` `Six Tasks` `Planning`
- [MIT Method (Most Important Tasks)](https://zenhabits.net/purpose-your-day-most-important-task/) - Time management strategy popularized by Leo Babauta focusing on identifying 1-3 most important tasks each day. Emphasizes doing MITs first thing in the morning during peak cognitive hours for maximum impact. ([Read more](/details/mit-method.md)) `Prioritization` `Productivity` `Morning` `Impact`
- [Pareto Principle (80/20 Rule)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-life-hacks/organization/understanding-pareto-principle-of-time-management) - Concept by Vilfredo Pareto stating that 80% of outcomes result from 20% of causes. In time management, 20% of efforts produce 80% of results, guiding focus on high-impact activities. ([Read more](/details/pareto-principle.md)) `Productivity` `Prioritization` `Efficiency` `Focus`
- [Parkinson's Law](https://asana.com/resources/parkinsons-law) - Observation by Cyril Northcote Parkinson (1955) that work expands to fill the time available for its completion. Applied to time management through strategic deadline setting and timeboxing. ([Read more](/details/parkinsons-law.md)) `Productivity` `Time Management` `Deadlines` `Efficiency`
- [Pomodoro Technique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomodoro_Technique) - Time management method developed by Francesco Cirillo that uses a timer to break work into 25-minute intervals separated by short breaks. Research shows it improves focus and reduces mental fatigue. ([Read more](/details/pomodoro-technique.md)) `Productivity` `Focus` `Methodology` `Time Management`
- [Time Blocking](https://www.timeblockplanner.com/) - Time management technique popularized by Cal Newport where you divide your day into blocks and assign specific tasks to each block. - [Timeboxing Method](https://clockify.me/timeboxing) - Goal-oriented time management technique that allocates fixed time periods for tasks. ([Read more](/details/timeboxing-method.md)) `Productivity` `Time Management` `Planning` `Methodology`
- [Two-Minute Rule](https://gettingthingsdone.com/2020/05/the-two-minute-rule-2/) - GTD principle by David Allen stating that if an action takes less than two minutes, do it immediately. - [Warren Buffett's 5/25 Rule](https://friday.app/p/5-25-rule) - Prioritization exercise attributed to Warren Buffett where you list 25 goals, circle your top 5, and actively avoid the remaining 20. ([Read more](/details/1-3-5-rule.md)) `Productivity Method` `Task Management` `Prioritization`

## Practices

- [Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/flsa) - The Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) is a U.S. federal law that sets minimum wage, overtime pay, recordkeeping, and youth employment standards. For time tracking, it defines how working hours and overtime must be recorded and paid, making accurate, auditable time and attendance tracking systems essential for compliance. Time tracking tools used in U.S. organizations must support reliable capture of hours worked, visibility into overtime, and long-term retention of editable history to satisfy FLSA requirements and reduce risk of non-compliance and payroll disputes. ([Read more](/details/fair-labor-standards-act-flsa.md)) `Compliance` `hr` `attendance`
- [SSAE 18 (Statement on Standards for Attestation Engagements No. 18)](https://windes.com/ssae-18-compliance/) - SSAE 18 is an attestation standard used for auditing and reporting on the controls of service organizations, such as cloud-based time tracking and attendance providers. Vendors that undergo SSAE 18/SOC examinations demonstrate that they maintain appropriate controls over security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy. For time tracking, choosing an SSAE 18-audited provider helps ensure that employee time data is securely stored, properly backed up, and reliably available, supporting compliance, auditability, and disaster recovery requirements.
